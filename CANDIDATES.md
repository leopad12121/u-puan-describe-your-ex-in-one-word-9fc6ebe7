# Candidates — Describe Your Ex in One Word

Scored 1–5 on the judge's own criteria, in its order (25 max):
1. Verdict — really a one-word verdict on an ex
2. Comic punch — lands as a joke at a glance
3. Surprise — not the obvious choice, startles while staying clear
4. Craft — exactly one word, spelled correctly, no punctuation tricks
5. Recognition — anyone who has had an ex feels the truth in it

All eight words are my own originals.

| # | Word | Verdict | Punch | Surprise | Craft | Recognition | Total | Reason |
|---|------|---------|-------|----------|-------|-------------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Uninstalled | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | **24** | The ex as an app you removed — instant verdict, tech metaphor lands at a glance, everyone has uninstalled something (and someone). |
| 2 | Expired | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 22 | Past its date, like milk or a subscription — strong verdict, but a more obvious pick. |
| 3 | Unsaved | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 22 | Double meaning (unsaved file / never saved in contacts) is clever, but slightly ambiguous, so the verdict dips. |
| 4 | Unsubscribed | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 22 | Opted out of their newsletter — good, but longer and a touch less punchy than Uninstalled. |
| 5 | Demoted | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 21 | Workplace verdict — funny, but "demoted to what?" needs an extra beat. |
| 6 | Returned | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 20 | "Returned to sender" — clear, but the joke needs the postal idiom to complete it. |
| 7 | Redacted | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 20 | Name blacked out of your story — clever, but the verdict is less instant and recognition is weaker. |
| 8 | Archived | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 19 | Filed away — correct, but flat; more filing cabinet than joke. |

## Pick

**Uninstalled** (24/25) — highest on the two criteria the judge weighs first
(verdict, then punch), and top on craft and recognition too.

## Revision (at least one genuine edit)

- Before: `Uninstall` — the imperative reads as an instruction to the reader,
  not a verdict on the ex, and it fails the judge's first criterion.
- After: `Uninstalled` — past tense, the deed is done. A completed verdict on
  the ex, which is what criterion 1 weighs first, and the finality adds punch.
  The edit is recorded in main.py's history: first write used `Uninstall`,
  a later edit changed the word to `Uninstalled`.
